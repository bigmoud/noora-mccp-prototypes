Spine and slots: one approved timeline, six different women

Every week, each woman gets the clinically approved message for her pregnancy week (the spine). What we know about her fills optional slots that change the wording, never the schedule. Pick a woman and a week to see what she receives, what Noora knows, and why.

The spine never waitsThe approved, EDD-based message goes out every week, with or without answers. Unknown means default wording, never "no".
One question per messagePicked from a priority queue. Skipped if Noora already knows the answer. Asked at most twice, two weeks apart, then dropped.
Every question has its own reply codesSo a late or out-of-order answer (“A” three weeks later) still lands in the right slot. Free text is read for facts, too.
Safety can pause contentBleeding pauses stretches until a nurse clears her. A passed due date with no birth news pauses pregnancy content and asks “Has your baby arrived?”
